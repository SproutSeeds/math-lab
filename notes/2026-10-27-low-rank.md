# PPT states of low rank on ℂ² ⊗ ℂᵐ in Lean

Date: 27 October 2026. Labels: Exact / Verified / Heuristic / Conjecture (ORP).

## Result (Verified)

Write `ρ_B = ρ₀₀ + ρ₁₁` (`ChainLowRank.redB`) for the reduced operator of ρ on ℂᵐ.

- `ChainLowRank.sep_of_rank_le_rank_redB`. For every m: a PPT operator ρ on ℂ² ⊗ ℂᵐ with
  `rank ρ ≤ rank ρ_B` is separable (Kraus, Cirac, Karnas and Lewenstein 2000).
- `ChainLowRank.rank_redB_le`. Every PPT operator on ℂ² ⊗ ℂᵐ has `rank ρ_B ≤ rank ρ`. So the hypothesis
  above says the two ranks are equal (`sep_of_rank_eq`).
- `ChainLowRank.sep_of_rank_le`. The form with full support: if ρ is PPT, ρ_B is invertible and
  `rank ρ ≤ m`, then ρ is separable.
- `ChainLowRank.rho24M_sharp`. The rank condition is sharp. P. Horodecki's state on ℂ² ⊗ ℂ⁴ (b = 3/5) is
  PPT and entangled. Its rank is 5, and its ρ_B has rank 4. `ChainPPT.rho24M_ppt_entangled` states the
  first two facts in the vocabulary of `Duality.lean`.
- Two Woronowicz lemmas are now stated for every dimension:
  - `ChainWoronowicz.exists_shear`: a shear makes the corner block invertible.
  - `ChainWoronowicz.exists_compress`: if `ρ_B w = 0` with `w ≠ 0`, then ρ lives on ℂ² ⊗ w^⊥.

  The 2 × 3 lemmas of the Woronowicz proof are now their special cases.

Definitions:

- rank is Mathlib's `Matrix.rank`, the dimension of the range;
- `IsPPT` and `IsSeparable` are those of `Duality.lean`, unchanged.

The statements are not vacuous. `rho24M_sharp` exhibits a PPT operator with `rank ρ = rank ρ_B + 1`
that is not separable. So the theorem's hypothesis is exactly what separates the two cases.

## The proof, file by file

| Step | Statement | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The corner form `[[1, B], [Bᴴ, C]]` has rank at least m. Its first block column, `u ↦ (u, Bᴴ u)`, is injective | `ChainTheory/LowRank.lean`: `cornerCol`, `le_rank_corner` |
| 2 | Rank m forces `C = Bᴴ B`. A nonzero Schur complement adds the vector `(0, (C - Bᴴ B) v)`, which lies outside that column | `LowRank.lean`: `eq_of_rank_corner_le` |
| 3 | With `C = Bᴴ B`, B is normal, and a normal corner is separable. Details below | `LowRank.lean`: `sep_of_normal`, `sep_of_corner_rank` |
| 4 | When ρ_B is invertible, one of the shears `z = 1, …, m + 1` makes the corner block invertible, and `1 ⊗ (Fᴴ)⁻¹` makes it 1. Details below | `ChainTheory/Woronowicz.lean`: `exists_shear`; `LowRank.lean`: `exists_corner_form` |
| 5 | Local operations do not raise the rank. So when ρ_B is invertible, ρ has rank at least m, and rank m makes it separable | `LowRank.lean`: `rank_lop_le`, `le_rank_of_det_ne_zero`, `sep_of_rank_le` |
| 6 | When `ρ_B w = 0` for some `w ≠ 0`, ρ lives on `ℂ² ⊗ w^⊥ ≅ ℂ² ⊗ ℂᵐ⁻¹` with the same rank and the same rank of ρ_B. Induct on m | `Woronowicz.lean`: `exists_compress`; `LowRank.lean`: `rank_redB_le`, `sep_of_rank_le_rank_redB` |
| 7 | Sharpness, explained below | `ChainTheory/PPTCriterion.lean`: `rho24M_ppt_entangled`; `LowRank.lean`: `rho24M_sharp` |

Step 3 runs as follows.

- PPT gives `C ⪰ B Bᴴ`. With `C = Bᴴ B`, the matrix `Bᴴ B − B Bᴴ` is positive and has trace zero, so it
  is zero and B is normal.
- A normal corner gives a separable operator. This is Woerdeman's criterion with the trivial dilation.

Step 4 runs as follows.

- If no shear works, there are m + 1 kernel vectors, one for each z. They are pairwise C-orthogonal and
  linearly independent, so they cannot all fit in ℂᵐ.
- So some shear makes the corner block invertible. Then `1 ⊗ (Fᴴ)⁻¹`, for a Gram factor F of that block,
  makes it 1.

Step 7 runs as follows.

- Horodecki's 2 × 4 state is PPT: both it and its partial transpose are rational sums of rank-one terms.
- It is entangled: the Choi matrix of the positive integer map φ₂₄ takes the value −1 on it.
- Its ρ_B has rank 4, since ρ_B has an explicit inverse.
- Its rank is at most 5, since it is a sum of five rank-one terms. Step 6 then forces the rank to be
  exactly 5.

## Where it departs from the paper

- **The partial transpose.** Kraus, Cirac, Karnas and Lewenstein take it on the qubit; ours is on the
  second factor. The two differ by a full transpose, which keeps positivity, so PPT means the same.
- **Rank by subspaces.** The rank facts come from one explicit subspace of the range (step 1) and one
  vector outside it (step 2). No rank formula for block matrices is used.
- **Sharpness from the theorem.** In `rho24M_sharp` the lower bound `rank ≥ 5` is not computed. It follows
  from the theorem, because the state is entangled.

## Limits

- Only ℂ² ⊗ ℂᵐ. The low-rank results for general ℂᴹ ⊗ ℂᴺ (Horodecki, Lewenstein, Vidal and Cirac 2000)
  are not attempted.
- `IsSeparable` asserts that a decomposition exists. The proof constructs one in principle, through the
  spectral theorem for normal matrices, but the decomposition is not extracted as data.
