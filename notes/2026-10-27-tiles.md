# A PPT entangled state of rank four in Lean

Date: 27 October 2026. Labels: Exact / Verified / Heuristic / Conjecture (ORP).

## Result (Verified)

- `ChainTiles.tiles_ppt_entangled`. The "tiles" state on ℂ³ ⊗ ℂ³ is PPT, is not separable, and has rank
  exactly four (Bennett, DiVincenzo, Mor, Shor, Smolin and Terhal 1999).
- `ChainTiles.exists_prodVec_of_isSeparable`. The range criterion (P. Horodecki 1997), in kernel form: a
  nonzero separable operator has a nonzero product vector orthogonal to its kernel. It proves entanglement
  with no witness map.
- `ChainTiles.tiles_unextendible`. The five tiles form an unextendible product basis: if a product vector
  `x ⊗ y` is orthogonal to all five, then `x = 0` or `y = 0`.

The tiles are the product vectors `ψⱼ = aⱼ ⊗ bⱼ`:

| j | aⱼ | bⱼ |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | `|0⟩` | `|0⟩ − |1⟩` |
| 1 | `|2⟩` | `|1⟩ − |2⟩` |
| 2 | `|0⟩ − |1⟩` | `|2⟩` |
| 3 | `|1⟩ − |2⟩` | `|0⟩` |
| 4 | `|0⟩ + |1⟩ + |2⟩` | `|0⟩ + |1⟩ + |2⟩` |

The state is `ρ = (1 − Σⱼ ψⱼψⱼ†/‖ψⱼ‖²)/4`, the normalized projector onto the complement of the tiles. Its
entries are rational.

This is the entangled half of the minimal-rank question. PPT states of rank at most two are already
separable (`ChainReduction.sep_of_rank_le_two`). Rank three is the next step on the roadmap.

## The proof

All steps are in `ChainTheory/Tiles.lean`.

| Step | Statement | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Range criterion. Details below | `exists_prodVec_of_isSeparable` |
| 2 | Unextendibility. The five orthogonality conditions are products, such as `x₀ (y₀ − y₁) = 0`. A case split leaves fifteen cases, each closed by one linear combination, because any three of the aⱼ (or of the bⱼ) span ℂ³ | `tiles_unextendible` |
| 3 | PPT. `ρ = Σᵢ wᵢ vᵢ vᵢᵀ` for four orthogonal integer vectors `vᵢ` with positive weights, and ρ equals its partial transpose because every ψⱼ is real | `isPSD_tiles`, `pt_tiles`, `isPPT_tiles` |
| 4 | Entangled. `ρ ψⱼ = 0` for all five tiles, and ρ ≠ 0 (trace one). By steps 1 and 2, a separable decomposition would contain a nonzero product vector orthogonal to the tiles, which cannot exist | `tiles_mulVec_tile`, `not_isSeparable_tiles` |
| 5 | Rank four. Details below | `rank_tiles` |

Step 1 runs as follows.

- Write ρ as a sum of product projectors.
- If `ρ v = 0`, then `v† ρ v = 0` is a sum of the terms `|⟨xₛ ⊗ yₛ, v⟩|²`, so each of them vanishes.
- Some `xₛ ⊗ yₛ` is nonzero, because ρ ≠ 0.

Step 5 runs as follows.

- The rank is at most four, because there are four rank-one terms.
- The rank is at least four, because the principal block on `(0,0), (0,2), (1,0), (1,1)` has an explicit
  integer inverse.

## Limits

- One example only. Other unextendible product bases, and PPT entangled states of rank four in other
  dimensions, are not stated.
- The minimal-rank statement needs rank-three separability. That is the next roadmap item: the M × N
  low-rank theorem of Horodecki, Lewenstein, Vidal and Cirac (2000).
